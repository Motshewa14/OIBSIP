About Us

More than just coffee. It's a vibe.

👯‍♀️

How it all started: Two besties. One burnt batch of beans. And a dream that wouldn't quit. We started this shop because we wanted a place where everyone belongs — where you can be weird, be loud, be quiet, or just be yourself.

We didn't have a plan. We just had coffee, books, and a whole lot of chaos. And somehow, that chaos turned into community.

We are about good vibes, good energy, reading books, listening to music, and speaking to friends over a cup of coffee.

We believe in creating a community that you can trust and have fun with. Whether you're sitting alone enjoying a book, or laughing with your besties — this is your space to just live life and enjoy yourself to the fullest.

✨ What Makes Us Different

📞

The Teller Booth

Our signature feature. A cozy little booth with a phone where you can vent about the book you just read. Cry, laugh, scream — we don't judge. We just listen.

📖

Books Are Free

Come with your own book or borrow one of ours. We have a library of stories waiting for you. No membership. No fees. Just pure reading joy.

🎵

Curated Vibes

Every playlist is handpicked to match the mood — chill, uplifting, nostalgic, or just whatever feels right. Music that makes you feel at home.

💕

More Than Coffee

We are active when it comes to community. We host book clubs, open mic nights, and "vent sessions" where you can just let it all out. No judgment. Just love.

📞

The Teller Booth — Our Heart

We know that sometimes, a book hits you right in the soul. You finish it and you just need to talk about it. But maybe you're alone. Maybe your friends haven't read it yet. Maybe you just need to vent.

That's why we created the Teller Booth. A private little space with a vintage phone. You pick it up, and you speak. About the book. About how it made you feel. About life. About anything.

We don't record. We don't judge. We just listen. Because sometimes, that's all you need.

Come with your book. Come with your feelings. Come exactly as you are. We're here for all of it. 💕

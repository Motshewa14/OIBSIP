The Accidental Coffee Shop

It all started on a broke Tuesday at 2 AM. Two besties, a second-hand espresso machine that sounded like a dying whale, and a bag of beans we definitely couldn't afford.

We just wanted to make coffee for our friends. That's it. Nothing fancy. Just something warm to share while we complained about life, love, and our questionable life choices.

Our first batch was burnt. Our second batch was watery. Our third batch? We accidentally set off the smoke alarm and had to explain to the neighbors that "no, we're not on fire, just passionate."

But our friends kept coming back. For the chaos. For the laughs. For the coffee that tasted like home — even when it was slightly burnt.

Then strangers started showing up. At first, we panicked. "Who are these people?!" But they ordered, they stayed, they smiled. And we realized — we accidentally made a coffee shop.

So we got serious. Well, serious-ish. We studied everything — bean origins, brewing temperatures, milk ratios, latte art (we're still working on that one). We watched tutorials at 3 AM, took notes on napkins, and practiced until our fingers were stained with coffee.

We learned that coffee is science. But it's also magic. It brings people together. It starts conversations. It turns strangers into regulars, and regulars into friends.

Now we're here — a little less broke, a little more caffeinated, and still making happy little accidents with extra foam. 💕

We didn't plan this. But we're so glad it happened. Because every cup tells a story. And we're honored to be part of yours. ☕
